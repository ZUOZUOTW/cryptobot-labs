# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome contributors of different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or pressure anyone
to disclose financial or account details.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
security reports, release channels, and other spaces where someone represents
CryptoBot Labs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through the contact channel on the owner's GitHub profile.
Do not include credentials or unrelated personal information.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, repetition, and safety when deciding
an appropriate response.
